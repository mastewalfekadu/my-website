Introduction

Coffee is grown in many parts of Ethiopia. The altitude, soil and climate of each region change the taste of the beans. Below are some of the best-known coffee regions.

Comparison of Regions

Main coffee-growing regions of Ethiopia
Region Altitude (metres) Flavour Notes Processing
Yirgacheffe 1,700 - 2,200 Floral, lemon, tea-like Washed
Sidama 1,500 - 2,200 Berry, citrus, sweet Washed and natural
Harar 1,500 - 2,100 Blueberry, wine, chocolate Natural
Jimma 1,400 - 2,000 Earthy, spicy, full body Natural
Guji 1,800 - 2,300 Peach, jasmine, bright Washed and natural

Washed or Natural?

Washed coffee
The fruit is removed from the bean before drying. This gives a clean, bright taste.
Natural coffee
The whole cherry is dried in the sun with the bean inside. This gives a sweet, fruity taste.
