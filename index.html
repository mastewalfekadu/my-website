Welcome

Ethiopia is known as the birthplace of coffee. Wild coffee plants still grow in the forests of the south-west, and coffee is part of daily life in almost every Ethiopian home. This website introduces the main coffee-growing regions and the traditional coffee ceremony.

The Legend of Kaldi

According to a popular legend, a goat herder named Kaldi noticed that his goats became full of energy after eating red berries from a certain bush. He tried the berries himself and felt the same effect. The story spread, and people began to roast and brew the beans.

Why Ethiopian Coffee Is Special

  • It has thousands of natural coffee varieties.
  • Most coffee is grown by small farmers, often without chemicals.
  • Each region gives the coffee its own unique flavour.
  • Coffee is a symbol of friendship and respect.
