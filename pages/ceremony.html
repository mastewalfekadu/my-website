What Is the Coffee Ceremony?

The coffee ceremony is an important tradition in Ethiopia. Family, friends and neighbours gather to talk while coffee is prepared from green beans. A full ceremony can take more than an hour.

Steps of the Ceremony

  1. Fresh grass and flowers are spread on the floor, and incense is burned.
  2. Green coffee beans are washed.
  3. The beans are roasted in a flat pan over charcoal until they are dark.
  4. The roasted beans are shown to the guests so they can smell them.
  5. The beans are ground with a wooden mortar and pestle.
  6. The coffee is boiled in a clay pot called a jebena.
  7. The coffee is poured into small cups called sini.

The Three Rounds

Coffee is usually served in three rounds. Each round has a name:

The three rounds of the coffee ceremony
Round Name Strength
First Abol Strongest
Second Tona Medium
Third Baraka Lightest, said to bring a blessing

What Is Served With Coffee?

  • Popcorn (fendisha)
  • Roasted barley (kolo)
  • Bread (dabo)
